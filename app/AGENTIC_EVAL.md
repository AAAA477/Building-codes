## (c) Safety test (fake LLM)

`tests/test_agentic.py::test_safety_invented_items_dropped` uses a fake LLM that returns an out-of-menu area (NBC:99.99), an invented article number (9.8.99.99) and a fabricated quote for a real article (9.8.5.4). Result: the area is dropped from the route, the invented article is dropped, the fabricated quote is dropped (zero results). `test_real_quote_survives_and_is_substring` shows a genuine quote is kept. Both pass.

## Method notes and limits

- Quotes are verified as substrings of the article text after whitespace normalisation; a quote that differs only in punctuation/case is re-anchored to the real span of the article text (still a substring), otherwise dropped.
- Cross-referenced articles (parsed from 'Article X' text, or from kg.py when present) are added without an LLM call; their quote is the article's first sentence and they are labelled not separately judged.
- Honesty note: in the first full run 2 of 36 (a) searches and 3 of 30 (b) searches fell back because the Azure client's one-time cold start (~45 s on this Windows box) was counted against the 45 s budget; agentic.py now excludes client start-up from search time and those searches were re-run once (no other searches were re-run or selected).
- Read budget is 6 full articles per search (the judge sees at most 1800 chars of each); large articles may be judged on a truncated view.
- The LLM routing menu has section titles only; a vague description can route to the wrong area.
