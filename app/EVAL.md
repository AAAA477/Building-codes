## Honest failures and limits

- No seeded defect missed.
- `D.doc.*` 'not marked as requiring a registered professional' fires for any unprovided document on a single-exit-stair topic; some legitimate non-professional documents (for example a maintenance plan) will be flagged for confirmation, so precision on that check is below 100%.
- The checker cannot judge whether content is adequate or correct, by design.
