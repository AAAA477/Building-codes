# Surface brief: Variance Workspace (applicant flow and reviewer view)

## Scope and visitor mode
Operate. Whole app shell: home, four-step applicant flow, reviewer view. Desktop browser, 1280px and up.

## Audience, job, task
Applicant first (inexperienced builder, contractor, manufacturer): prepare a complete variance application and submit it. Secondary: Safety Codes Officer triaging a file in ten minutes. Constraints: no government, Safety Codes Council or event logos; completeness only, never safety claims; AI output always labelled as a draft; humans sign.

## Chosen direction
Tabbed code binder (challenger rw-manual-acetate-tab-board, won both axes against the assigned triplicate-permit-form world). Signature move: a stepped tab rail down the left edge, one tab per step or form section, each in its own full-strength hue, tab height proportional to the work still open, and the open tab extending to become the board the page sits on. Raised by FAC catalog (only the active section at full saturation) and the split-flap board (a state change restyles a row without breaking the grid). Unresolved: exact hues and type faces, chosen by the builder within the contract and checked for AA contrast.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The workspace is a tabbed reference binder. The tab rail is the app's map, progress meter and navigation at once. It refuses the stepper-plus-white-cards SaaS default.
OWN-WORLD: Section hues at full strength on the board (oxide orange, chrome yellow, grass, teal, ultramarine, violet; vermilion reserved for errors and errata slips) with a milk-white leaf on top. A bookish text face near 17/26 at a 62-68 character measure, a plain grotesque for labels and heads, mono for article numbers and attribution codes. Selection is a punched-hole mark; errors are a vermilion errata slip. No gradients, glass or soft shadows; one short hard shadow on the leaf edge.
STORY: In one glance the applicant sees where they are (the open tab), how much is left (tab heights) and the next action. They believe a citation because its source line (article, page, quote) is printed under it. They end by downloading the filled form and a submission package.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left tab rail about 72px wide with one tab per step (Describe, Rules, Fill and review, Submit), heights proportional to open items; the current tab extends in its hue into a strip running along the top of a milk-white leaf. The leaf holds the step heading in the grotesque, the content at reading measure, and article cards with a verified source line; a bottom bar carries Back and the primary Next action at bottom right. Reviewer view reuses the rail with form sections A, B, C, D and Sign.
FORM: Operate world from the roll (key a6b388a2): rw-manual-acetate-tab-board, challenger, verdict wins. Motion: two-frame 90ms steps, no easing, none under reduced motion.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
