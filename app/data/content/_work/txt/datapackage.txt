City of Calgary, Guide to Applying for an Alternative Solution (Variance) 
City of Calgary, building advisories, bulletins and Standing Posted Variances. Calgary publishes Standing Posted Variances (SPVs) that any builder can reference on a permit rather than applying individually — currently covering backwater valves, firewalls in rowhouses with secondary suites, exposed building face construction, ventilation, and zero lot line. The procedure exists because 26 builders were filing the same zero lot line variance repeatedly: 382 requests in eight months of 2017, each separately written, issued and forwarded to the Safety Codes Council.
City of Edmonton, Guideline for Alternative Solutions Affecting Emergency Services (April 2025). Guidance for alternative solutions that touch firefighting operations, which the NBC(AE) requires applicants to consider without clearly defining. These submissions are peer-reviewed by Safety Codes, Permits and Inspections together with Edmonton Fire Rescue Services.
City of Edmonton, Point Access Block Alternative Solution Guide (2025). A structured pathway for single-stair buildings, with 13 design studies. Shows an authority having jurisdiction pre-defining the shape of an acceptable argument for a recurring innovation category.
City of Edmonton, Application Requirements for House Permits. See the “Policy Solutions” section for a lists the policy solutions Edmonton offers for conditions that repeatedly require a variance — including B23-01 on fire separation between row houses with more than two secondary suites, B23-02 on window opening control devices, B23-03 on roof truss engineering, and B23-05, a policy interpretation on the application of Article 9.9.4.4.

NRC Publications Archive. The main free corpus of Canadian building and fire science research — occupant egress studies, tenability analyses, residential fire characterization, full-scale burn testing.
CCMC Registry of Product Assessments. Lists evaluated construction products with the standards and code editions they were assessed against, and is available through a JSON API. Directly relevant to the product-specific path and to determining whether an evaluation already exists for a given product.

Copyright
NRC codes and guides are free to download but remain copyrighted. This package links to them rather than redistributing them. Teams intending to embed code text in a commercial product should review NRC's terms directly — a real consideration for the business plan, not a formality. Alberta STANDATA are published under the Open Government Licence – Alberta and may be redistributed.
Edition control
All work should reference the second printing of the NBC(AE) 2023.
