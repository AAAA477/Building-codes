PART 3 – Option to Appeal Written Refusals 
The applicant (owner and/or owner's representative), to whom Refusal of Proposed Alternative Solution applies, may 
appeal it to the Safety Codes Council (SCC) in conformance with SCC Bylaws. All appeals must be made within 30 
days of the date the Refusal was served. 
Section A: Reviewer Information 
A request for an Appeal shall: 
•
be in writing, in the form of the Council’s Notice of Appeal and signed by the appellant,
•
contain a copy of Part 1 and 2(b) of Variance Form for Proposed Alternative Solution,
•
contain a concise statement concerning the matter being appealed setting out the grounds for appeal,
•
contain the name, address, telephone number, and email of the person appealing; and
•
be accompanied by the appropriate fee set out in the Council fee schedule.
Note: The appeal process is not available to those applications that have been reviewed and refused by an 
Administrator of the Safety Codes Act.
