National Energy Code of Canada for Buildings (NECB) 2020 Online Training – Navigating and Applying the Code

Share:
Self-paced online training to help professionals across the building sector navigate Canada’s 2020 National Energy Code for Buildings (NECB) with clarity and ease.

Published: 2023

Organizations: Natural Resources Canada, National Research Council of Canada, and CSA Group

Format: Online Training

Duration: 30 minutes

Access the Training here (free registration required)

Why We Recommend It?

Getting to know the 317-page National Energy Code of Canada for Buildings 2020 (NECB) can be daunting and time-consuming. Developed by Natural Resources Canada, the National Research Council of Canada, and CSA Group, this free, self-paced video offers clear and concise guidance on how to navigate and use the NECB so you can quickly locate the information you need, when you need it. The training helps NECB users save time and increase productivity by learning how to more effectively find and apply code requirements.

Next Steps & Related Reading...

  • Curious how the industry can benefit from updated codes? Explore our Business Case and Case Study sections to learn how meeting higher-tier codes can yield benefits beyond just energy cost savings.
  • Concerned about the cost of adopting higher-tier codes? Visit our Funding & Financing Resources section to discover support for your low-carbon projects.
  • Would code-specific workshops be helpful to you? We’re considering future deep-dive training sessions—let us know if this interests you by contacting us.
